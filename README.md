# Privacy Policy for Arrow Out

**Last Updated:** August 20, 2026

Welcome to **Arrow Out** ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by **Muhammad Abdullah Wali**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how our game handles user data, permissions, and privacy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Data
**Arrow Out does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).**
- We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or account credentials.
- The game can be played 100% offline without creating an account or logging in.

### 1.2 Non-Personal & Local Data Storage
All game progress, including:
- Unlocked level numbers,
- Star ratings earned per level,
- In-game coin balances,
- Theme customizations,
- Sound effect and haptic feedback preferences,

are stored **locally on your device** using Android `SharedPreferences` / iOS local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or cloud database.

---

## 2. Device Permissions

Arrow Out is designed to require minimal device permissions:
- **Vibration / Haptic Feedback (`android.permission.VIBRATE`):** Used solely to deliver tactile haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g., button taps, block launch pops, and mistake thuds).
- **No Invasive Permissions:** Arrow Out does **not** access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, storage files, or device identifiers.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

- Arrow Out does **not** integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or behavioral analytics trackers.
- No personal information is sold, rented, traded, or shared with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Out is a family-friendly logic puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages, including children under 13:
- We do **not** knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
- If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

---

## 5. Data Deletion & Reset

You have full control over your game data:
- **In-Game Reset:** You can reset all level progress, coins, and star ratings at any time via the **Settings > Reset Progress** button inside the game.
- **Uninstalling:** Uninstalling or clearing the app cache/data via your device's Android/iOS Settings will permanently erase all local game data.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
